# VH3rd-Remastered-Rebalanced

This configuration package is a Rebalance of the Vault Hunters 3rd Edition - Remastered Modpack.

This rebalanced configuration targets the v4.0.1 version of Vault Hunters Remastered.  Previous releases for v2.0.4 and v3.0.1 are also available.  Future balancing changes will only be targeting the v4.0.1 or newer versions.

# New v4.0.1 Changes

Integrated the previous v3.0.1 rebalance changes into the updated v4.0.1 modpack configuration.  Removed deprecated loot items that are no longer used in Vault Hunters Remastered v4, and adjusted several loot tables to account for balance changes in the stock modpack.  Previous Jewel modifier and Rune Boss overrides were also removed, which will use their stock v4.0.1 configurations.

## Vault Recycler

The stock v4 modpack removed the drop chance for Netherite Scrap & Faceted Focus from the Vault Recycler.  The Rebalanced config retained the drop chance, but kept it a very rare bonus drop.

- Netherite Scrap has a 5% drop chance from Magnets, Vault God Charms and Vault Gear
- Faceted Focus has a 2.5% drop chance from Non-Legendary Vault Gear

## Champions Loot Table

Redesigned the loot table for Champions, retained the regular Vault Gear items but added additional bonus rare drops. 

- Champions Loot (Levels 0-49) added: jewel pouch, bitter lemon, pog, mod box, mango, trinket
- Champions Loot (Level 50+) added: jewel pouch, bitter lemon, pog, mod box, mango, sour orange, echo gem, trinket

# Loot Balance Changes

Listed below are the major rebalance changes where an item's expected drop rate has changed ±10% or more. This accounts for both how frequently an item is selected from the loot table and the quantity received when it drops.

Items with smaller changes are not listed, as their expected drop rates remain mostly the same as the stock modpack. Vault Hunters uses a weighted loot system, so increasing the drop weight of one item can reduce the chances of other items in the same loot pool. I've tried to avoid that as much as possible, though some reductions are intentional. For changes of ±50% or more, I've also listed the approximate chance of the item appearing in a chest to provide additional context.

### Wooden Chests Loot
- Drift Wood: 15% increase
- Vault Plating: 24% increase
- Vault Essence: 14% increase
- Silver Scrap: 14% increase
- Magic Silk: 19% increase
- Carbon Nugget: 19% increase
- Shulker Shell: 31% decrease
- Mod Box: 42% increase

### Wooden Barrel (Level 50+ ~0.78% chance, or about ~1 in 128 Wooden Chests can spawn as a Wooden Barrel)

These are very rare Wooden Barrels that spawn after level 50, with an increased chance of containing some of the rarer Wooden Chest items along with a few extras!  Below are all the items that can drop from a Wooden Barrel.

- Chromatic Iron Ingots
- Carbon Nuggets
- Vault Plating
- Mod Boxes
- Vault Catalyst Fragments
- Silver Scrap
- Vault Essence
- Wooden Chest Scrolls
- Chromatic Steel Ingots

- Vault Diamond: 14% increase

### Coin Piles
- Vault Coins: 33% average increase for Bronze, Silver & Gold

### Completion Crates
- Rebalanced Completion Crates to provide more consistent rewards and reduce the chance of low-value or empty reward rolls.
- Split gear reward pools with multiple rolls into separate Armor and Weapon/Accessory pools.
- Added a small amount of guaranteed Vault Bronze to early-game Completion Crates.
- Added a chance for Magnets to appear in Completion Crates.
- Slightly increased the amount of Vault Gold received.

### Elixir Objective
- Vault Chests: Increased the chance of higher Elixir rewards from Wooden, Living, Gilded, and Ornate chests.
- Coin Piles: Increased Elixir rewards across all levels, adding higher reward tiers of up to 5 rolls.
- Vault Ores: Increased the chance of higher Elixir rewards from mining ores.
- Vault Dwellers: Increased the chance of higher Elixir rewards from killing Dweller mobs.

### Bingo Objective Changes
- Decreased Loot Chest and Mine Block objective requirements by 15%
- Decreased Discover Omega Room objective requirements for all difficulty levels.
- Increased all Timed objectives (Average weighted increase for all timed objectives is ~73%)

### General Vault Changes
- Scavenger Vault: Reduce Scavenger Item requirements by 30%.
- Reduced the frequency of the special Holiday themes, so they weren't so heavily weighted in October and December.
- Increased the drop chance of Catalyst Fragments, they can also very rarely drop from Gilded, Living or Ornate chests.
- Increased drop chance of some items in Challenge Rooms rewards.
- Increased the ore spawning slightly in alt theme Vaults.
- Increased success rate for Imbuement Altar.
- Increased the likelihood of Challenge Rooms and Omega Rooms in Chaos Vaults.
- Small increase to Digsite, Mine and Warehouse Omega Room chances.
- Increased Augment drop rate to 25% from Completion Crates.
- Increased the Relic, Particle & Ancient Relic drop chance by 20%.

### Mob Changes
- Guardian Mobs: Reduced attack damage by 20% and maximum health by 15%
- Guardian Mobs: Reduced attack reach by 30% for Guardians that have an increased reach attribute.

### Vault Item Changes
- Increased the minimum uses for Charms to 10.
- Increased the minimum uses from 12 to 15 for Crafted Trinkets.
- Added a few mod items to Mob Boxes from Powah and increased the drop chance on a several rare items.

### Other Changes
- Increased the drop chance of some of the rarer Catalysts in the Shop Pedestal & Black Market.
- Small tweaks to Black Market item prices.
- Small tweaks to abilities: Frost Nova, Glacial Blast & Scatter Javelin
